Ground troubleshooting procedures in both the original page image and its extracted text; preserve the original diagram alongside translated steps.

**Why:** The uploaded LG service excerpt contains branching Yes/No diagrams and variant-specific test values. Linear PDF text extraction loses branch geometry, so text-only generation can reverse a decision or attach a test to the wrong variant. A valid quotation is provenance, not proof that the generated procedure is technically correct.

**How to apply:** Keep original source pages available in the app and reports, label generated steps as support for qualified service personnel, and require model/voltage/condensation applicability checks. Do not turn hypotheses into confirmed fault codes or treat this service excerpt as a complete manual.
